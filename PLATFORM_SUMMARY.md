## 🎓 How to Use This Platform

### **Best Learning Sequence:**

1. **Start with Learning Section**
   - Read through each disorder systematically
   - Review concept maps and clinical relationships
   - Take notes on key features and nursing care

2. **Move to Quizzes**
   - Quiz by category first (genetic, behavioral, infectious, abuse)
   - Mix all categories once comfortable
   - Focus on learning rationales, not just getting answers right

3. **Study the Rationales**
   - This is where deep learning happens
   - Understand WHY each answer is correct/incorrect
   - Connect concepts to your prior nursing knowledge

4. **Use Music for Study Sessions**
   - Click Spotify or YouTube links
   - Stream music while reviewing content
   - Find what helps YOUR concentration

5. **Review Resources**
   - Watch related YouTube videos for visual learning
   - Reference textbooks for deeper understanding
   - Connect platform content to classroom material

## 🧠 Evidence-Based Learning Strategy

This platform uses research-proven techniques:

- **Spaced Repetition:** Review material over time for long-term retention
- **Retrieval Practice:** Active recall is more effective than passive reading
- **Elaboration:** Connect new information to existing knowledge
- **Interleaving:** Mix different topics and question types to prevent surface learning
- **Clinical Application:** Case studies and Bowties teach real-world nursing
