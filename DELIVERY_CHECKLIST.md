## 🎓 Educational Philosophy

This platform is built on research-backed learning principles:

1. **Spaced Repetition** - Return to material multiple times over time
2. **Retrieval Practice** - Active recall strengthens memory
3. **Elaboration** - Connect new info to existing knowledge
4. **Interleaving** - Mix different topics and question types
5. **Clinical Application** - Apply concepts to real patient scenarios
6. **Metacognition** - Understand WHY answers are right/wrong
