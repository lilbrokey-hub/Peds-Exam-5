## 📋 Suggested Study Sequence for Maximum Learning

### Week 1-2: Genetic Disorders
1. Down Syndrome - understand how extra chromosome → developmental changes
2. CF - understand how gene mutation → thick mucus → multiple system effects
3. Sickle Cell - understand how mutation → polymerization → vaso-occlusion
4. Hemophilia A - understand how factor deficiency → bleeding problems
5. DMD - understand how dystrophin loss → muscle degeneration

### Week 3: Behavioral/Developmental
1. ASD - understand neurodevelopmental differences
2. ADHD - understand dopamine dysregulation
3. ODD - understand emotion regulation dysfunction
4. Conduct Disorder - understand severe impairments

### Week 4-5: Infectious Diseases
1. Measles - viral spread and immune response
2. Pertussis - bacterial toxins and coughing
3. Varicella - viral spread in crops
4. Meningitis - bacterial CNS infection
5. TB - chronic mycobacterial infection

### Week 6: Abuse/Neglect
1. Physical - intentional trauma effects
2. Sexual - exploitation and trauma
3. Neglect - failure to provide basics

### Throughout All Weeks
- Watch YouTube videos for visual reinforcement
- Read textbook sections for depth
- Review concept maps to see relationships
- Study to foundational concepts
