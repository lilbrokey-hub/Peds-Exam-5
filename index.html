SECTION 1 • GENETIC

Care of the Child with Genetic Disorders

Watch Ninja Nerd Lectures
🧬
Trisomy 21

Down Syndrome (Trisomy 21)

Chromosomal Abnormality

Extra chromosome 21 (full trisomy), translocation, or mosaicism.

Risk Factors (from PDF)
Maternal age >35 or paternal >50
Decreased fetal movement
Abnormal ultrasound/amniotic fluid
Associated Conditions (PDF)
• Congenital heart defects
• Hypothyroidism
• Increased leukemia risk
• Chronic otitis media
• GI anomalies
Clinical Pearls + Nursing Interventions
Lifelong multidisciplinary care. Screen for cardiac defects immediately. Early intervention for speech & motor delays improves adaptive functioning. Key Pearl: Hypotonia is universal — focus on positioning and feeding safety.

Other Key Genetic Disorders

  • Turner Syndrome (45,X)
    Female, short stature, webbed neck
  • Klinefelter Syndrome (47,XXY)
    Male, tall, hypogonadism
  • Fragile X
    Most common inherited intellectual disability
  • PKU
    Autosomal recessive metabolic disorder
  • Neurofibromatosis
    Café-au-lait spots, neurofibromas

Significant Medical History Findings (PDF)

  • • Maternal age >35 / paternal >50
  • • Repeated premature births, breech delivery
  • • Congenital hip dysplasia
  • • Abnormal prenatal ultrasound / blood tests
  • • Exposure to teratogens
Learning Science Tip: Genetic disorders are multifactorial — always assess family history + prenatal exposure together.
SECTION 2 • BEHAVIORAL & DEVELOPMENTAL

Care of the Child with Behavior, Cognition or Developmental Disorders

Intellectual & Developmental Disorders

Limitations in intellectual AND adaptive functioning (social interaction, language, self-care).

Lifelong challenges requiring health care + educational support
Umbrella term now includes Autism Spectrum, Cerebral Palsy, Sensory Deficits (ADA)
🧩

ADHD

Inattention, hyperactivity, impulsivity. Evidence-based: behavioral therapy + stimulants when needed.

Ninja Nerd-style: dopamine dysregulation
🔄

Autism Spectrum Disorder

Social communication deficits + restricted/repetitive behaviors. Early diagnosis = better outcomes.

🍷

Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder (FASD)

Preventable lifelong neurodevelopmental disorder caused by prenatal alcohol exposure.

No safe amount of alcohol during pregnancy

Terminology & Causes (Direct from PDF)

  • • Mental age vs. Functional age
  • • Adaptive functioning
  • • Intellectual disability (replaces “mental retardation”)
  • • Autism spectrum disorders
Causes of Intellectual Disability
  • 🧬 Genetic
  • 🤰 Pregnancy alterations
  • 🍼 Neonatal alterations
  • 🦠 Acquired childhood diseases
  • 🏡 Environmental problems
SECTION 3 • INFECTIOUS

Infectious Disorders in Pediatrics

🧼

The #1 Way to Prevent Spread of Disease

HAND HYGIENE (PDF emphasis)

Key Terminology (PDF)

Incubation Period: Time from exposure to first symptoms
Prodromal Period: Early nonspecific symptoms
Communicability: Period during which the person can transmit the agent

Chickenpox / Varicella (Classic Example)

Description (PDF): Rash begins as macules → vesicles → crusts. Virus remains latent (can cause shingles later).

Etiology: Varicella zoster virus

Pearl: Highly contagious via respiratory droplets + direct contact. Vaccination prevents most cases.
Macules → Vesicles → Crusts
Remains in body for life

SECTION 4 • CHILD ABUSE

Child Abuse & Neglect

Manifestations (Direct from PDF)

Physical Indicators
  • • Bruises in various stages of healing
  • • Bite marks, burns in unusual locations
  • • Skeletal injury
Behavioral Clues
  • • Wariness or fear of adults
  • • Resists going home
  • • Inconsistent stories (child vs. parent)

Includes (PDF)

Emotional abuse Physical abuse Sexual exploitation / molestation Neglect

Neglect = deliberate failure to provide physical, educational, or emotional needs

Risk Factors for Child Maltreatment (PDF)

  • Perpetrator alcohol or drug abuse
  • Domestic abuse in the home
  • Child with disability
  • Socioeconomic factors: economic hardship, lack of resources
No single etiologic factor — interaction of multiple risks
